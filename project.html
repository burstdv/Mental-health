Welcome

Welcome to Mental Health with Nature. This website will help you discover peaceful nature areas, in Wellington where you can relax, and stay calm taking care of your wellbeing.

Explore our map of relaxing nature spots, or read more about why we created this website on our About Us page.

Our Aim

Our aim is to help people who struggle with mental health by providing them a service that would connect them with peacful spaces that would help with their wellbeing.
